AstroHistory privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

AstroHistory is an Android app published by mphaxise, with the package name app.astrohistory.week. It shows calculated sky positions and recorded history. The app makes no claim about future events. It is for personal information and entertainment.

What the app collects

AstroHistory collects no personal data. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics service. This version sends nothing off your device and makes no network connection.

What stays on your device

The app keeps a count of which screens were opened. The count sits in the app's private storage on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it. Nobody else receives it.

Permissions

The app declares the internet permission. This version leaves it unused.

Sharing

The app shares no data, because it collects none.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
